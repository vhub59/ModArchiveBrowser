Author's Comments:

Nei's port of Renoir's Act III fitted to TBSE.

Replaces: Academic's Coat, Breeches and Longboots
Fits: Midlander, Highlander, Elezen, Au Ra, Miqo'te & Viera

Not dyeable.
Comes with an additional darker look for the pants.

I tried my best fitting everything as close to the original as possible. Weights aren't perfect but I tried.
Like always, there might be clipping from your Idles, c+, personal animations etc., you know the drill.
Tested for general movement.

The outfit can be sort of mixed with other tops or bottoms, there is some lower clearance on the vest due to how the pants sit. There's skin mesh up to the navel since the vest doesn't sit skin tight on the hips for the above reason.
